What I assumed

Three separate consumer-facing sample apps, not the Go console. Each is a fictional customer brand running a Go hosted journey, so the shell is the customer's and the verification steps are ours. Copy follows Go's plain-English, British-spelt register.

Why the three markets differ

Banking carries full KYC and AML, so it earns a four-step flow with a referral branch. Healthcare adds proxy registration and explicit record-sharing consent. Gaming is a speed play: silent age and data checks first, documents only as a step-up.

Open questions

Are these hosted journeys or SDK builds? Hosted caps a page at five fields, which changes the Details step. Also: which markets and regions per app, and whether the sample brands should be neutral or match named prospects.
